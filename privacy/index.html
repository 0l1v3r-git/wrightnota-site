WrightNota Privacy Policy

Effective: October 12, 2026 · Last updated: October 12, 2026

Local work data and accounts

WrightNota requires no WrightNota account. Work records, notes, checklists, Context Memory, timestamps, photo references and Service Report display settings are stored locally on your device. WrightNota has no developer-operated server for uploading, storing or synchronizing this content. The app does not send these records to the developer.

Camera and Photos

With your permission, WrightNota uses the camera to take Work Capture photos and saves them to Apple Photos. WrightNota keeps references to those photos, rather than a separate permanent archive of original images in its work database. Photos access lets you view linked photos and Service Report pages and organize the optional WrightNota Album. Limited permissions or deleted originals can make linked images unavailable.

Photo processing and document-page adjustments happen on your device. WrightNota V1 does not use the microphone, record audio or request location access.

Apple services and device settings

Apple Photos, iCloud Photos and device backups are controlled by your system settings. Apple may synchronize, back up or retrieve content; WrightNota can request linked photos from iCloud through Apple Photos. Local-first does not mean that Apple receives no information or that every photo is always available offline. You can review or revoke Camera and Photos permissions in iOS Settings.

Analytics, tracking and sharing

WrightNota V1 has no advertising, analytics, third-party tracking or developer-operated crash-reporting SDK. It does not transmit work content, location or device identifiers to a developer telemetry service. The developer does not collect or share your local work content through the app. Apple may process platform diagnostics according to your settings and its terms.

Lifetime Pro and purchases

Lifetime Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. WrightNota uses verified purchase entitlement information on your device to unlock unlimited Works and restore eligible purchases. There is no developer receipt-upload server. WrightNota does not receive your payment-card details. Apple processes transactions and may provide the developer with App Store sales and transaction reporting under its own policies.

Keeping and deleting content

Local records remain until you delete them or remove the app’s local data. Ordinary Work deletion leaves its Context and source photos. Only an explicit option to delete photos also requests removal from Apple Photos. Deleting a Context detaches its Works without deleting those Works or their photos. Deleting a Service Report grouping leaves its member Captures and source photos.

Removing the app’s local data does not itself remove Photos assets or Apple purchase history. Apple manages photo synchronization, backups and Recently Deleted; deletion in WrightNota does not guarantee removal from all Apple services.

This website

This static website has no analytics, tracking pixels, third-party scripts, forms or cookies set by the site. It uses system fonts and local CSS. GitHub Pages hosts it; GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Hosting logs and their retention are governed by GitHub’s Privacy Statement. See also GitHub Pages hosting information.

Privacy questions and support

The dedicated WrightNota support email is not yet available. This page will be updated when the contact channel opens. Visit the Support page for current guidance. WrightNota does not automatically upload support material. When a contact channel becomes available, avoid sending confidential customer records or unnecessary work photos.

Policy updates

If the app or website’s data practices change, we will update this policy and its last-updated date on this page.

现场记隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 12 日 · 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 12 日

本地工作数据与账户

现场记无需注册账户。工作记录、备注、检查清单、对象备注、时间信息、照片引用及服务报告显示设置保存在设备本地。现场记没有开发者运营的服务器来上传、存储或同步这些内容，应用不会将这些记录发送给开发者。

相机与照片

获得许可后，现场记使用相机拍摄工作照片，并将照片保存在 Apple 照片图库。应用保留这些照片的引用，不在工作数据库中另存一套永久原图档案。照片权限用于查看关联照片和服务报告页面，以及整理可选的现场记相簿。有限权限或原图被删除可能使关联图片无法显示。

照片处理与文件页面显示调整在设备本地进行。现场记 V1 不使用麦克风，不录制音频，也不请求定位权限。

Apple 服务与设备设置

Apple 照片、iCloud 照片和设备备份由你的系统设置控制。Apple 可能同步、备份或取回内容；现场记可以通过 Apple 照片请求取回关联的 iCloud 照片。本地优先不表示 Apple 不接收信息，也不保证所有照片始终可离线查看。你可以在 iOS 设置中查看或撤销相机与照片权限。

分析、跟踪与共享

现场记 V1 不含广告、分析、第三方跟踪或开发者运营的崩溃上报 SDK，不通过开发者遥测服务发送工作内容、位置或设备标识。开发者不会通过应用收集或共享你的本地工作内容。Apple 可能根据你的设置和平台条款处理系统诊断信息。

终身专业版与购买

终身专业版为一次购买的非消耗型项目，通过 Apple App Store 与 StoreKit 处理。现场记在设备上使用经验证的购买权益，解除工作数量限制并恢复符合条件的购买。应用没有开发者运营的购买凭据上传服务器，不会接收你的银行卡信息。Apple 负责交易处理，并可能按其政策向开发者提供 App Store 销售与交易报表。

保留与删除

本地记录保留至你删除记录或移除应用本地数据。普通工作删除会保留关联对象与原始照片；只有明确选择同时删除照片，应用才会另行请求从 Apple 照片中删除原图。删除关联对象会解除其与工作的关联，不删除工作或照片。删除服务报告分组会保留其中的拍摄记录与原始照片。

移除应用本地数据本身不会删除图库照片或 Apple 购买记录。照片同步、备份与「最近删除」由 Apple 管理；应用内删除不保证所有 Apple 服务中的副本均已清除。

本网站

本静态网站没有分析工具、跟踪像素、第三方脚本或表单，也不设置 Cookie。网站使用系统字体与本地 CSS，由 GitHub Pages 托管；GitHub 会出于安全目的记录访问者的 IP 地址。托管日志及其保留方式适用 GitHub 隐私声明，另见 GitHub Pages 托管说明。

隐私问题与支持

现场记的独立支持邮箱尚未开放。联系渠道开放后，我们会更新本页。 请查看支持页面上的现有帮助。现场记不会自动上传支持资料。联系渠道开放后，请避免发送客户机密记录或不必要的工作照片。

政策更新

应用或网站的数据处理方式变化时，我们会在本页更新政策及最后更新日期。
