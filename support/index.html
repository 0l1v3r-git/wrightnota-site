WrightNota Support

What is WrightNota, and who is it for?

WrightNota is a local-first field-work memory tool for technicians, engineers, service and maintenance professionals, consultants and others doing technical work on site. Capture photos with Before / After, Old / New and Sequence context, keep work notes together, and recall useful details on your next visit.

How do I start?

Open New Work, optionally choose a Context and name, then go to Capture. The first successfully saved Capture establishes the Work. A meaningful saved Work Note can also establish a Work. Use Current Work to return to the work at hand and Record to revisit previous Works.

Do I need an account? Where is my data?

No WrightNota account is required. Work records are stored on your device; capture photos are saved to Apple Photos and linked by reference. WrightNota has no developer-operated cloud sync. Apple Photos and device backup settings may involve Apple services.

What does Free allow?

Free lets you retain up to 5 Works simultaneously, with no cumulative lifetime limit and no time limit. Existing records remain accessible. Deleting a Work frees capacity so you can create another.

What does Lifetime Pro unlock? Is it a subscription?

Lifetime Pro unlocks Unlimited Works. It is a one-time, non-consumable purchase, with no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Use Settings → Purchase Status → Restore Purchases, or Restore Purchases on the Lifetime Pro purchase screen. Use the Apple account that holds the eligible purchase. Apple handles purchase authentication and billing. If restoration fails, check your connection and App Store account, then try again.

Why Camera and Photos permissions?

Camera access is for taking Work Capture photos. Photos access is for saving captures, viewing the exact linked photos and Service Report pages, and organizing the optional WrightNota Album. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings.

Does WrightNota use the microphone?

WrightNota V1 does not use the microphone or record audio.

Why is a photo unavailable?

Check that the original still exists in Apple Photos and is included in WrightNota’s Photos permissions. An iCloud-only photo may need a connection to download. With Limited Photos access, use the app’s photo-access controls to include the linked photo. WrightNota does not keep a separate permanent archive of source photos.

What happens when I delete a Work?

Ordinary Work deletion removes its app content while retaining its Context and original photos. Deleting photos is a separate explicit choice. Deleting a Context detaches associated Works without deleting their content. Apple manages Photos synchronization, backups and Recently Deleted.

How can I contact support?

The dedicated WrightNota support email is not yet available. This page will be updated when the contact channel opens. When the channel opens, include the screen, iOS version, app version and what happened. Avoid customer photos, serial numbers and private notes unless necessary. Never send Apple passwords or payment-card details.

Where can I read the privacy policy?

Read the WrightNota Privacy Policy for local storage, permissions, purchases and website hosting details.

现场记支持

现场记是什么？适合谁？

现场记是一款本地优先的现场工作记录工具，面向技术人员、工程师、服务工程师、维护技师、顾问，以及其他在现场做技术工作的人。拍照时标记前后、旧新与序列，把工作备注放在一起，让下次到访时有据可查。

如何开始？

打开「新建工作」，可选关联对象与名称，然后进入「拍摄」。第一张成功保存的照片会建立工作；保存有实际内容的工作备注也可以建立工作。用「当前工作」回到手上的工作，用「记录」回看以往工作。

需要账户吗？数据保存在哪里？

无需现场记账户。工作记录保存在设备上，拍摄照片存于 Apple 照片图库，应用保留关联引用。现场记没有开发者运营的云同步服务。Apple 照片与设备备份可能根据你的系统设置使用 Apple 服务。

免费版可以保留多少工作？

免费版最多同时保留 5 个工作，没有累计创建总量限制，也没有使用时限。已有记录仍可查看；删除工作后会腾出名额，可以继续创建新工作。

终身专业版解锁什么？是订阅吗？

终身专业版解除工作数量限制。工作数量不限。一次购买。它是非消耗型购买项目，无需订阅。

如何恢复购买？

进入「设置 → 购买状态 → 恢复购买」，或在终身专业版购买页面选择「恢复购买」。使用持有符合条件购买的 Apple 账户。购买认证与计费由 Apple 处理。恢复失败时，请检查网络与 App Store 账户后重试。

为什么请求相机与照片权限？

相机权限用于拍摄工作照片。照片权限用于保存拍摄照片、显示准确关联的照片与服务报告页面，以及整理可选的现场记相簿。你可以在 iOS 设置中管理权限。

会使用麦克风吗？

现场记 V1 不使用麦克风，也不录制音频。

为什么照片无法显示？

请检查原图是否仍在 Apple 照片中，以及是否包含在现场记获准访问的照片范围内。仅存于 iCloud 的照片可能需要联网下载。使用有限照片权限时，可通过应用内的照片访问操作加入关联照片。现场记不另存一套永久原图档案。

删除工作会怎样？

普通工作删除会移除应用内的工作内容，但保留关联对象与原始照片。同时删除照片需要明确选择。删除关联对象只解除它与工作的关联，不删除工作内容。照片同步、备份与「最近删除」由 Apple 管理。

如何联系支持？

现场记的独立支持邮箱尚未开放。联系渠道开放后，我们会更新本页。 联系渠道开放后，请说明所在页面、iOS 版本、应用版本和发生了什么。除非必要，请勿发送客户照片、设备序列号或私人备注。不要发送 Apple 密码或银行卡信息。

在哪里查看隐私政策？

请阅读现场记隐私政策，了解本地存储、权限、购买与网站托管。
